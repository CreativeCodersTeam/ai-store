We got a report against yargs-parser 18.1.0 (this repo, checked out at the release commit). A downstream user builds a CLI where some of the arguments come from a webhook payload. They say that after parsing, completely unrelated objects in their process suddenly carry extra properties — e.g. `({}).isAdmin === 'true'` — and their auth middleware then lets everyone through. Their minimal repro:

```js
const parser = require('yargs-parser')
parser('--config.__proto__.isAdmin true')
console.log(({}).isAdmin)   // 'true'
```

Our test suite is green (329 tests, `npx mocha test/*.js`). The repo is at <project>. Find out what is going on, how deep this goes, and what our options are for fixing it without breaking downstream users.
